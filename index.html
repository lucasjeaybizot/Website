About me

I am a Postdoctoral Scientist at Cedars-Sinai Medical Center working in the Bonda lab studying volition and cognitive control with single-neuron recordings in pediatric and adult recordings. I completed my PhD in Computational and Data Sciences at Chapman University under the supervision of Dr. Aaron Schurger at the LUCID lab studying the readiness potential primarily with EEG.

My research focuses on human volition and decision-making. I am particularly interested in how neural activity and environmental context constrain when and how decisions are made.
